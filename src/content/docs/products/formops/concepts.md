FormOps is intended to help teams manage reusable groups of questions for native JSM Forms. A **component** is a reusable group of questions; a **version** identifies a particular revision; and an **association** describes a relationship to a form. These concepts and their behavior remain subject to development.

Review the [component library](/products/formops/component-library/) and [synchronization](/products/formops/synchronization/) pages for further context.
