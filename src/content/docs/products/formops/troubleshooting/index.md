FormOps is under development, so supported configurations and product-specific troubleshooting steps are not yet verified. Synchronization and native form publishing must not be assumed to work.

If you encounter an issue in an authorized preview, record the environment and sanitized reproduction steps, then use the [bug report guide](/support/report-a-bug/). Do not send credentials or customer information.
