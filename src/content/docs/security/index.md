Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to [security@roman-devworks.com](mailto:security@roman-devworks.com). Please follow our [vulnerability reporting guidance](/security/vulnerability-reporting/). Confirm this email alias before public launch.

## Security practices

Share only the information needed to investigate an issue. Avoid sending credentials or customer data. Product-specific security details will be published when verified and approved.

## Privacy

A privacy policy and terms of service are pending approval and are not published as active policies. For privacy questions, contact [contact@roman-devworks.com](mailto:contact@roman-devworks.com); confirm the alias before public launch.
